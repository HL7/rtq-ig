Response 2 · follow-up questions
quality-4.1
The chromatograms for batch B-002 at 6 months accelerated (40°C/75% RH) show an unidentified peak at RRT 0.85 at 0.12%, above the 0.10% identification threshold. Identify this degradation product and propose a specification limit.
The peak at RRT 0.85 is the N-oxide of the active substance, identified by LC-MS and confirmed against a synthesized reference standard. It forms only under accelerated conditions in the Alu/Alu pack. Proposed shelf-life limit: NMT 0.15%.
quality-4.2
Provide the toxicological qualification of this degradation product per ICH Q3B(R2), or justify why qualification is not required.
The N-oxide is a major human metabolite, so it is qualified at the proposed limit of 0.15% per ICH Q3B(R2). To confirm this, SynthPharma commits to a 13-week repeat-dose toxicity study on the isolated N-oxide.
🤝 Commitment offereddue 2026-09-30
Complete a 13-week repeat-dose toxicity study on the isolated N-oxide degradation product and submit the study report.
