admin-1Q1
Is the fee for the Type II variation fully paid and documented?

Yes, the fee was paid on June 25, 2025. Payment confirmation and receipt are included in the application form (see Section 1.1).

📎 Fee Payment Receipt

admin-2Q2
Are all required metadata fields in the application form complete?

Yes, all required metadata fields are complete: MAH (SynthPharma AG), product (ExampleDrug 10 mg Tablets), procedure (NRA/H/C/005432/II/0023), submission date (2025-06-25).

product-1Q3
Do the updated storage conditions (2–8°C for 36 months) require additional patient guidance?

No additional patient guidance is required. The ePI clearly states: 'Store in a refrigerator (2°C – 8°C). Do not freeze.' The 36-month shelf life is supported by stability data and does not alter patient handling instructions.

product-2Q4
Is the patient leaflet revised to clarify the extended shelf life?

Yes, the patient leaflet has been updated to state: 'Shelf life after first opening: 36 months when stored at 2–8°C'. The revised leaflet is included in the submission.

product-3Q5
Is the updated ePI text sufficiently clear for all applicable languages?

Yes, the ePI text has been translated into all applicable languages and reviewed for clarity. Status: English (Approved), French (Approved), German (Approved).

quality-1Q6
Are there any new stability studies conducted, or does the submission include only data from additional timepoints from previously submitted studies?

The submission includes both additional timepoints (long-term data extended to 36 months for primary batches A-001, A-002, A-003) and new stability studies (pilot batches B-001, B-002 in the new Alu/Alu pack, 6-month accelerated and long-term data). No protocol deviations occurred.

🤝 Commitment offereddue 2027-06-30
Submit the 48-month long-term stability results for batches A-001, A-002 and A-003 as a post-approval variation.
QuestionnaireResponse.item.extension: rtq-commitment
quality-2Q7
Does the data support all intended climatic zones?

Yes, the data supports all ICH climatic zones, including Zone IVb (30°C/75% RH long-term) per WHO TRS 1010. All batches remain within specification.

quality-3Q8
Are the test methods used the same as in the previously submitted stability information?

Yes, all test methods are identical to those validated in the original MAA. Methods remain stability-indicating for assay, impurities, and dissolution.

quality-4Q9
Are there any new degradation products identified within the newly submitted data?

No new degradation products were identified. All observed impurities correspond to previously qualified degradants, with levels below 0.10% (ICH Q3B(R2) threshold).

quality-5Q10
Any changes in the packaging configuration and/or storage of the samples during stability?

Yes, the pack changed from a PVC/PVDC blister to an Alu/Alu blister. New stability studies (B-001, B-002) were initiated in the Alu/Alu system. Storage conditions are unchanged.

product-3Not resolved
Is the updated ePI text sufficiently clear for all applicable languages?

Apologies for the omission. The approved French and German ePI texts (linguistic review completed 2025-12-12) are now included in the response package alongside the English text.

quality-4Further questions
Are there any new degradation products identified within the newly submitted data?
quality-4.1
The chromatograms for batch B-002 at 6 months accelerated (40°C/75% RH) show an unidentified peak at RRT 0.85 at 0.12%, above the 0.10% identification threshold. Identify this degradation product and propose a specification limit.

The peak at RRT 0.85 is the N-oxide of the active substance, identified by LC-MS and confirmed against a synthesized reference standard. It forms only under accelerated conditions in the Alu/Alu pack. Proposed shelf-life limit: NMT 0.15%.

quality-4.2
Provide the toxicological qualification of this degradation product per ICH Q3B(R2), or justify why qualification is not required.

The N-oxide is a major human metabolite, so it is qualified at the proposed limit of 0.15% per ICH Q3B(R2). To confirm this, SynthPharma commits to a 13-week repeat-dose toxicity study on the isolated N-oxide.

🤝 Commitment offereddue 2026-09-30
Complete a 13-week repeat-dose toxicity study on the isolated N-oxide degradation product and submit the study report.
